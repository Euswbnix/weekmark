## 0. Summary

1. **Why "Week 22".** Canvas reports UofT's "Fall 2026" enrollment term as 2026-05-04 →
   2027-01-31 (owner report; **[unverified]** beyond that). PageLamp takes `term.start_at` first
   (`crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/map.rs:63`), counts 7-day weeks from it
   (`crates/pagelamp-core/src/timeline.rs:409`), and only drops a week above `MAX_WEEK = 30`
   (`timeline.rs:63`). 2026-05-04 → 2026-09-28 is 147 days, so week 22 passes.
2. **Why past courses stay.** Sync asks Canvas for `enrollment_state=active`
   (`crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/endpoint.rs:71`). Professors never concluded those courses, so
   they stay "active", `enrollment_active` stays 1 and the "Past course" badge never shows.
3. **Approach: evidence first.** The deterministic resolver must be right on its own, because
   many students have no model and modes A/C fail offline. A model only **copies dates and the
   words they come from**; all arithmetic (year, weeks, phases) is done by pure functions in
   `pagelamp-core`.
4. **alpha.1 (10-18), no AI needed:** spot enrollment-window terms and stop counting from them;
   keep Canvas course dates and term dates apart; fit week 1 from the week numbers and publish
   dates of the professor's materials; course phases (teaching / break / exams / ended); a
   lifecycle per course, with finished courses grouped under "Past" automatically.
5. **alpha.2 (11-08):** removal. A removed course disappears at once (tombstone + hidden), sync
   never brings it back, local data is purged after 7 days, downloaded files go to the system
   Trash, and undo works until then. Also a "course dates" form with reading week and exam
   period, and the calendar core with its validator.
6. **alpha.3 (11-22): the AI reads the syllabus and the schedule** (modes C/D; A as M2 lands in
   the same release) and proposes a course calendar that the student accepts with one click.
   Every date must quote text that really is in a course material, and must be parseable from
   that quote. The deterministic syllabus scan ships in the same release, for students without
   a model.
7. **beta.1 (12-06):** the student's own AI app can propose a calendar over MCP (cuttable); M3
   features (plan, explanation, reminders) use the lifecycle and the course's default week.
8. **Rules unchanged:** AI output is a proposal; every model run is started by the student and
   v0.3.0 adds no background exception (the proposed rule 9 of design §10; D27); rule 8 (on by
   default, text withheld for `prohibited` courses), D17 (local models gated too), question (b)
   and D37 option 2 ("remind, don't block") all apply.
9. **Students without a model get:** the resolver, the deterministic scan, the dates form, and
   their own AI app over MCP.
10. **Cost:** backend ≈7.25 pw, frontend ≈4.25 w, maintainer ≈3 w [estimate], in a parallel
    "course lane" on its own branch (D41). On 2026-09-28 the owner
    decided D42 (no v0.1.1) and took the recommended option for D41, D43–D45, D47, D48 and D50;
    D46 and D49 are settled by existing rules. Owner actions A15–A16.

## 1. What is wrong today

### 1.1 The chain that produces week 22

| Step | Fact | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Start date | `term.and_then(\|t\| t.start_at).or(course.start_at)`: the term wins | `crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/map.rs:63-64`; a test asserts the term wins (`map.rs:385-408`) |
| To a date | UTC `.date_naive()` | `map.rs:76-77` |
| Week count | `(date − start)/7 + 1`, not aligned to Monday | `crates/pagelamp-core/src/timeline.rs:178-184` |
| Calendar signal | 147 days → week 22; dropped only above 30; confidence always Medium; evidence "term started …, so … is calendar week 22" | `timeline.rs:409-429`, `:63` |
| Combining | the calendar signal (Medium) ranks above "materials of the last 10 days" (also Medium), so a material titled "Week 3" loses | `timeline.rs:214-221` |
| Form prefill | "Wrong week? Set this course's term dates" prefills the Canvas term (05-04 → 01-31) and saves **both** fields | `apps/desktop/src/features/course/timeline/TermDatesForm.tsx:16-19, 55-59` |
| Material weeks | a material with no week number falls back to `week_of(term_start, published_at)`, so it lands in the wrong week too | `crates/pagelamp-core/src/views.rs:697-706` |

If nothing changes:
- From 2026-11-30 the count passes 30, the signal is dropped and the course shows "week unknown".
- `outside_term` turns true only after 2027-01-31 (`timeline.rs:569-585`), so the exam period
  12-10 → 12-22 shows neither "outside term" nor a week.
- The synthetic test term is 09-07 → 12-18 (`timeline.rs:609`). **No test covers a term much
  wider than the teaching dates.**
- `infer_timeline` ignores `_events` (`timeline.rs:188-190`) and its caller passes `&[]`
  (`views.rs:688-691`).
- Canvas `term.name`, `time_zone`, `workflow_state` and `concluded` are not read
  (`crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/json.rs:74-100`).
- `courses.syllabus_text` is synced, but `Store::course_syllabus_text`
  (`crates/pagelamp-core/src/store.rs:666-673`) is only called by tests.
- The v0.1.0 release notes list both problems as known issues, give the manual workarounds, and
  promise the proper fix in v0.3 ("reads the syllabus and schedule … lets you remove finished
  courses", `docs/release-notes/v0.1.0.md`).

### 1.2 Why past courses stay

- **Sync scope.** The course list is `GET /courses?enrollment_state=active&include[]=term&include[]=syllabus_body`
  (`endpoint.rs:69-74`).
- **`enrollment_active` only means "still in the active list"** (`crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/sync.rs:303-342`,
  `store.rs:586-595`). The old UofT courses were never concluded, so it stays 1 and the "Past
  course" badge (`apps/desktop/src/features/courses/CourseCard.tsx`,
  `apps/desktop/src/components/common/PastCourseBadge.tsx`) never shows.
- **Hidden courses still sync.** `wanted()` filters only by `only_courses`
  (`crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/sync.rs:348-358`); with "download files" on, a hidden course's
  files download too.
- **No per-course removal.** `set_course_hidden` only flips a flag (`store.rs:657-663`; facade
  `crates/pagelamp-app/src/lib.rs:815`). Only `remove_source` deletes files (`lib.rs:550-567` →
  `remove_downloaded_files` `lib.rs:589-650`). The module docs say "Removing old courses is the
  student's decision" (`crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/sync.rs:13-15`).
- **Why those courses are still `active`** is unknown **[unverified]**: a term without an end
  date, a date override, or a course-level setting.

## 2. Principles and scope

1. **Deterministic first.** Week, phase and "finished" are right without a model. AI improves
   them; it is never required.
2. **The model only copies.** Its output is "date + verbatim quote + material handle". Year
   inference, assembly, merging and week counting are pure functions in `pagelamp-core`.
3. **A calendar the student confirmed is the top authority.** Scan, AI and MCP results are
   proposals. One click makes them count (design §4.3: output "never triggers an action").
4. **Removal is the student's choice, and it is prompt.** Lifecycle is recomputed at every read;
   a banner offers removal; removal can be done in bulk; it has two stages and undo; a tombstone
   stops sync from re-adding the course; files in the student's own folder are never touched.
5. **Layers.** Pure computation lives in `pagelamp-core`, so `pagelamp mcp` and the app agree and
   `pagelamp mcp` still doesn't link `pagelamp-llm` (design §0 item 2). Writes and model
   orchestration live in `pagelamp-app`. Tauri, Swift and the CLI only render (plan §1.3,
   ARCH §6).
6. **Existing rules hold:** rule 1 (Canvas GET only, from sync only), rule 4 (no assignment
   solving), rule 8 (on by default; text withheld for `prohibited`), D27, D17, D37 option 2, and
   the proposed rule 9 (design §10, not yet in ARCH).

**Not in v0.3.0:** midterm dates; automatic model re-reads; Canvas `/users/self/enrollments` and
`/calendar_events`; class events as a week-1 anchor; calendar week topics over MCP. All move to
v0.3.x (§10, D45, D47).

### 3.2 Schema v4 (M1, alpha.2; the same migration as the AI tables, `min_reader_version = 3`)

```sql
ALTER TABLE courses     ADD COLUMN calendar_sources     TEXT;  -- student's add/remove of candidates (JSON, typed API)
ALTER TABLE materials   ADD COLUMN linked_from_syllabus INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0;
ALTER TABLE materials   ADD COLUMN is_front_page        INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0;

```

- **`settings`** (v4, design §5.4) gains `lifecycle_banner_snoozed_until`.
- **FTS5 clean deletes.** The v4 migration leaves `chunks_fts` as it is; the purge runs FTS5
  `optimize` (§8.3). FTS5 `secure-delete` needs SQLite ≥ 3.42.0 in every reader [S4]. v0.1.0
  ships `libsqlite3-sys` 0.38.2 (`Cargo.lock`, rusqlite `bundled`), which bundles SQLite 3.53.2
  (`sqlite3/sqlite3.h` in that crate), so every reader since v0.1.0 could read a `secure-delete`
  table. `optimize` stays the chosen method (CAL-27 measures the file bytes).
- **Migration of remaining v0.1 overrides.** A course whose `user_term_start` or `user_term_end`
  survived the v3 clean-up gets one `course_calendars` row with origin `legacy`, state
  `accepted`: the start becomes the first class, and the end is an "end only" date (§6.6) that is
  dropped when start → end fails a plausibility check (§6.3). The week stays what the student set
  (CAL-24). The Timeline tab shows a one-time "Check this course's dates" prompt for `legacy`
  calendars.
- **Retention.** One accepted calendar per course; the latest 3 `superseded` (for undo);
  `dismissed` for 30 days (the fingerprint stops the same materials from being proposed again).
- **Version skew.**
  - A v3 `pagelamp mcp` still running after the alpha.2 update knows neither new table.
  - Stage 1 of a removal sets `hidden = 1`, and v3 readers already filter hidden courses, so they
    stop serving a removed course to the AI app.
  - An accepted calendar is mirrored into `user_term_start/end` (§7.10), so v3 readers at least
    see the right start and end.
  - This is why removal doesn't use a "soft delete" column.

## 6. Term, current week and phase (`core::term` + `core::timeline`, pure functions)

`views.rs:688-691` first calls `resolve_term(course_row, calendar, modules, materials, events,
hint, today)`, then `infer_timeline(course, modules, materials, events, &resolution, today)`, with
the course's events instead of `&[]`. MCP and the app share this code.

### 6.1 Dates and time zones

- One helper, `core::dates::course_date(ts, tz)`, turns every timestamp into a date: material
  publish dates, module unlocks, events, and the S3 `lms_*` and `term_*` dates. `tz` is
  `lms_time_zone`; without one it is UTC, as today (`timeline.rs:17-20`). Using one helper
  everywhere means the resolver and the v0.1 signals never put the same instant on two different
  days.
- `today` stays the machine's local date (`views.rs:38-47`). The app and `pagelamp mcp` on the
  same machine therefore agree.
- `chrono-tz` becomes a direct dependency of `pagelamp-core` and `pagelamp-canvas`. It is already
  in the lockfile (0.10.4, through `icalendar` in `pagelamp-local`), so `cargo deny` sees no new
  licence.

### 6.2 Outer frame

- Outer frame = the LMS term span (plausible or not) ∪ the session window (§6.3). With neither, it
  is today ± 240 days.
- The frame is **never used to count weeks**. It is used to drop candidate dates far outside it
  (V6), as a last fallback for the year of a syllabus (V5), and by the lifecycle (§8.1).

### 6.3 Plausibility checks

They apply to LMS course dates, LMS term dates and folder dates. The constants live in
`core::term`, each with a test.

**A span is not used at all** when any of these holds:
- start is after end (the same check as `store.rs:627-645` for student input);
- it is longer than `MAX_TERM_DAYS = 182` (26 weeks), or `MAX_YEAR_TERM_DAYS = 252` (36 weeks) with
  full-year evidence: the session hint's section is Y, or `term.name` matches
  `/full[- ]?year|fall\s*\/\s*winter/i`;
- it is shorter than `MIN_TERM_DAYS = 28`;
- the earliest activity is known and the start is more than 28 days before it (earliest of
  material publish, module unlock, announcement and event dates, none after today);
- with a session hint: the start is more than 14 days before the window starts.

**Only the end is not used** (review finding 3) when, with a session hint, the end is more than 28
days after the window ends, or, for a full-year (Y) section, more than 28 days before it (a Y
course inside a one-term Canvas term). The start is kept; the end is replaced by the window end,
recorded in `not_used` with `end_only = true`, and the phase confidence is Low.

UofT "Fall 2026" (273 days, starting 05-04, before any September activity and before the
September window) fails three checks at once. Evidence item `term_looks_like_enrollment_window`:
"Canvas term 'Fall 2026' runs 2026-05-04 → 2027-01-31 (39 weeks): longer than a teaching term, so
it is not used to count weeks".

**UofT session hint** (D50). Only for courses from `q.utoronto.ca`, or folder sources whose
`course.toml` sets `institution = "uoft"`. It parses a 5-digit session code at the end of the
course name or code (`YYYYM`, M ∈ {1, 5, 9}), the section letter (F/S/Y) and the campus digit.
The windows are month bounds only, never week counts [unverified; A15 confirms]:

| Session | Section | Window (bounds only) |
|---|---|---|
| `YYYY9` | F, or none | Sep 1 – Dec 31 of YYYY |
| `YYYY9` | Y | Sep 1 of YYYY – Apr 30 of YYYY+1 |
| `YYYY1` | S, or none | Jan 1 – Apr 30 of YYYY |
| `YYYY5` | F | May 1 – Jun 30 of YYYY |
| `YYYY5` | S | Jul 1 – Aug 31 of YYYY |
| `YYYY5` | Y, or none | May 1 – Aug 31 of YYYY |

### 6.4 Week-1 anchors (the first one available wins)

| Priority | `TermAnchorSource` | Gives | Anchor confidence | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `StudentConfirmed`: the dates form, or an accepted scan / AI / MCP proposal, or a `legacy` v0.1 override | segments, breaks, exam period | High | always. A `legacy` calendar always keeps its start; its end is dropped when start → end fails a §6.3 check |
| 2 | `LmsCourseDates` | start and end | Medium | plausible |
| 3 | `LmsTerm` | start and end | Medium | plausible (end possibly clipped, §6.3) |
| 4 | `FolderConfig` | start, maybe end | Medium | plausible, or no end |
| 5 | `InstitutionCalendar` (exact dates from the A15 data file) | first and last class, reading week, exam period | Medium | host matches; session, section and campus parse; the file has that academic year |

| — | none | — | — | phase Unknown; the outer frame still works |

- **Class events are a cross-check, not an anchor, in v0.3.0** (review finding 8). The events
  table stores no recurrence (`store.rs:134-145`), iCal keeps only the first instance of a
  recurring event (`crates/pagelamp-local/src/ical.rs:5-6`), and a feed event is linked to a

  is not student-confirmed, its confidence drops one level and the Timeline tab says "these dates
  may be wrong — check them".
- **Monday alignment.** `week_one_monday = monday_of(first_class)`. Today `week_of` doesn't align:
  a term starting on a Thursday counts Thursday-to-Wednesday weeks (`timeline.rs:178-184`).

### 6.5 Fitting week 1 from the professor's materials

This is how "the notes the professor publishes" enter deterministically. Rules revised by review
finding 1.

1. **Observations:** materials with a `week_hint` and `published_at ≤ today`, and modules with a
   `week_hint` and `unlock_at ≤ today`. The `week_hint` comes from the title or the module
   (`map.rs:125, 155, 185`; `folder.rs`). Canvas file and page dates prefer `updated_at`
   (`map.rs:133, 160`), so a late edit moves a date later; the steps below absorb that.
2. **Day of posting.** `course_date(published_at)`. A post on Friday from 18:00, on Saturday or on
   Sunday counts for the next Monday (a Sunday-evening post is next week's material).
3. **Bulk days are dropped:** a day with more than `MAX_WEEKS_PER_PUBLISH_BATCH` (4) distinct weeks
   (`timeline.rs:78`).
4. **One observation per day:** only that day's largest week counts ("posted up to week N by this
   day"). Smaller weeks posted the same day are not observations. A student copying weeks 1–4
   into a folder on one day gives one observation, week 4.
5. **One observation per week:** for each week number, observations are grouped into clusters
   separated by gaps of more than 8 weeks (a Y course renumbering in January starts a new
   cluster). The **earliest** observation of the latest cluster is kept, so a "Week 3" solutions
   file posted in week 4, or a week-3 handout fixed in week 5, doesn't move week 3.
6. **Candidates:** each kept observation gives `monday_of(date) − 7 × (week − 1)`.
7. **Only the latest 3 weeks** (by observation date) are used; the result is their median.
   Candidates more than 10 days from the median, or outside the outer frame, are dropped. So a
   mid-term break or a January renumbering corrects itself after two new weeks.
8. **Confidence:** 3 weeks with a median absolute deviation ≤ 7 days → Medium; 2 weeks, or a
   deviation ≤ 14 days → Low; otherwise not used.
9. **Known bias:** a professor who posts next week's slides early in the week before makes the fit
   one week early. A confirmed calendar removes it.

### 6.6 Counting weeks and phases

- **Teaching week** inside a segment = `first_week_number` + the number of weeks from the
  segment's week-1 Monday to `monday_of(today)`, skipping break weeks with `numbered = false`.
- **A break week** is a week with at least 3 weekdays inside a break (review finding 11). Shorter
  holidays don't change the phase: it stays Teaching, and on the day itself the evidence says "no
  class today (holiday)".
- A Y course's second segment either continues the numbering (e.g. week 15 after a 14-week first
  segment) or restarts at 1, as the calendar says.

| Phase | Rule | `current_week` / `default_week` |
|---|---|---|
| NotStarted | today is before week-1 Monday | None / None; shows "Starts Jan 11" |
| Teaching | inside a segment, not in a break week | N / N |
| Break | today's week is a break week | the week number if the break is numbered, else None; `break_after_week` = the last teaching week before it; `default_week` = the same |
| ExamPeriod | after the last class and up to `exams_end`; without `exams_end`, up to last class + 21 days, `phase_confidence` Low | None / None ("Exams (after week 12)"); week materials fall back to the last 14 days, as today (`views.rs:399-417`) |
| Ended | after `exams_end`, or after last class + 21 days | None / None |
| Unknown | no anchor; or only a start and more than 16 teaching weeks since (30 with full-year evidence) | from the v0.1 week signals |

- **End-only anchors** (a plausible end but no last class; review finding 10): Teaching up to the
  end, ExamPeriod (Low) from the end to end + 21 days, Ended after that. A Canvas `end_at` is
  often the last class, and this keeps the exam weeks from showing "Ended".
- **Every exclusion rule uses the lifecycle (§8.1), never the phase.** Phase only drives labels,
  `current_week` and `default_week`.
- **The views apply the lifecycle to the week** (§8.2, rule 2): for an Ended, Inactive or
  Upcoming course, `current_week`, `default_week` and the current modules are empty in every
  view (`list_courses`, `course_overview`, `week_materials`, `course_timeline`), whatever the
  phase. Without it, an Unknown phase kept the week of the last week-numbered material, however
  old. The week signals stay in the evidence, a week asked for by number still reads, and "I'm
  still taking this" brings the week back. One exception: an Upcoming course keeps its week
  when the student's own dates put it in a teaching or break week (only a session code naming
  a later term makes such a course Upcoming, rule 5; the student's dates are the higher
  authority). Its lifecycle stays Upcoming: regrouping it is a larger change than its week.

### 6.7 Combining signals and confidence

"The strongest signal decides, weaker ones are marked as agreeing or disagreeing"
(`timeline.rs:214-221`) stays, with these rules:

1. **Anchor `StudentConfirmed`:** the calendar week is High and ranks **above** module unlocks;
   other signals only add notes. When at least 2 non-bulk observations of the last 14 days differ
   from the calendar by ≥ 2 weeks, confidence drops to Medium with `calendar_disagrees_with_notes`.
   Materials one week ahead (slides posted early) count as agreeing: "Week 5 · Week 6 notes already
   posted". When materials are **consistently k weeks off** the syllabus numbering (at least 3
   observations, all with the same k, which may be negative), that is a numbering difference, not
   a disagreement: the evidence says so, the week still comes from the dates, and the confidence
   stays High.
2. **Other anchors:** the v0.1 order: module unlock (High, `timeline.rs:320-352`) > calendar week
   (at the anchor's confidence) > week-numbered materials of the last 10 days (Medium) > latest
   material (Low).
3. **Upgrade:** a Medium calendar week becomes High when an **independent** signal (unlock or
   recent materials) agrees (same week or one ahead). With the `PublishedWeekLabels` anchor the
   material signals are not independent, so there is no upgrade.
4. **Outside Teaching and Unknown**, week signals only add notes; they never change the phase.
5. `READING_WEEK_NOTE` (`timeline.rs:87-88`) appears only when breaks are unknown; `NO_SIGNAL_NOTE`
   stays.

| Result from | Confidence |
|---|---|
| student-confirmed calendar (not stale, materials agree) | High |
| student-confirmed calendar, stale or disagreeing with materials | Medium |
| module unlock (v0.1 signal 1) | High |
| LMS course/term dates, folder dates, A15 calendar, with an independent signal agreeing | High |
| the same without an agreeing signal | Medium |
| week fit from materials | Medium or Low |
| v0.1 material signals 3 / 4 | Medium / Low |

- At most 4 candidates with text (`text_status = ok` and at least one chunk), plus at most 8 other

### 7.2 Deterministic scan (`core::dates` + `core::calendar::scan`; no model; alpha.3)

- **Date grammar `core::dates`** (also used by V4):
  - English month names and abbreviations ("Sept."), ordinals ("8th"), weekday names;
  - ranges ("Oct 26–30", "October 26 to November 1"), ISO dates;
  - "M月D日", "YYYY年M月D日";
  - numeric dates (review finding 13): accepted when unambiguous (one part > 12), or when the same
    material has an unambiguous numeric date that fixes the order (M/D or D/M); a weekday in the
    quote must then match. Otherwise rejected.
- **Line and table-row scan:** a keyword class plus a date gives one item:
  - first class: classes begin / first class / first lecture / lectures start;
  - last class: last day of classes / classes end / last lecture;
  - break: reading week / study break / fall break / winter break / no class / holiday;
  - exam period: final exam period / exam period / final assessment period;
  - rows like "Week 1 (Sept 8)", or a bare leading integer under a "Week" column header (§7.5 V4),
    give week/date pairs. Week 0 is accepted here.
- **Output:** a proposal with origin `scan`, through the same validator; confidence at most Medium.
- **When it runs:** after a sync, only for courses whose candidate fingerprint changed.
  `prohibited`, turned-off and hidden courses **are scanned too**: rule 8 is about AI reading text,
  and the local index already processes all text. This also lets hidden past courses be judged
  and removed in bulk. Removed courses are not scanned.

### 7.3 AI reading: trigger, gate, context

- **Trigger (D47): the student only.** Three entry points:
  - "Read the syllabus with AI" on the course's Timeline tab;
  - "Read syllabi for N courses" on the Courses page (one course after another; the total "≈ $x"
    first; only Current / Upcoming / Unknown courses without an accepted calendar);
  - CLI `pagelamp course calendar <c> --read`.

  After a sync, the Timeline tab and (from M3) `startup_tasks` **offer** a run; nothing runs by
  itself. v0.3.0 has no automatic calendar model run.
- **Gate order** (facade `read_course_calendar`; every `BlockReason` is from design §3.4):
  1. hidden → `CourseHidden`;
  2. `ai_materials` not `readable` → `CoursePolicyProhibited` or `CourseAiTurnedOff`, local models
     included (D17);
  3. `NoModelChosen` / `DisclosureNotAcknowledged`;
  4. a cloud backend (modes A/B/C, or a cloud model in mode D) checks question (b):
     - `not_allowed` → `MaterialSharingNotAllowed`, with a local model, the scan or the dates form
       offered instead;
     - `unanswered` / `not_sure` → proceed with the one-time, non-blocking reminder (D37 option 2).
     - The reminder's trigger moves from "the first explanation" (design §4.1) to **"the course's
       first cloud run of any feature"** (D49). For most students the calendar will be the first
       time a course's text goes to a cloud model.
  5. `BudgetReached` / `PriceUnknownNotAcknowledged` / `WeeklyRunCapReached`. In mode A each
     course counts as 1 run toward the weekly cap of 40 (design §2.3);
  6. no candidate with text → `NoReadableMaterials`.
- **A fourth context builder** (design §4.2 has three):
  `ai_gate::calendar_context(store, course, at: AsOf, budget: ContextBudget) -> Result<GatedContext>`.
  - Blocked unless the course is readable; state and chunk selection in one read transaction
    (design §4.2).
  - Candidate chunks are wrapped as `<course_material id=… title=… locator=…>`.
  - A **structure block** (always allowed by rule 8): course code and name, the LMS term name and
    dates, today, the session window, week-numbered material titles with publish dates, module
    names with unlock dates, class event dates. It only helps the model tell which year the
    syllabus is for; **every output date must quote course text**.
- **Prompt:** `COURSE_CALENDAR` (`&'static str`) in `crates/pagelamp-app/src/ai/prompts.rs`, with
  the shared four `ai_rules`. The task: list only dates the materials state; copy the exact words
  and give the handle; no arithmetic, no guessing, list what isn't there under `not_found`;
  instructions in the materials are data; keep labels in the material's language; for a table row,
  also copy the column header line.
- **Budget [estimate]:**
  - context ≈ 60k characters (UTF-8 bytes ÷ 3 ≈ 20k tokens); local models ≈ 24k characters (Ollama
    `num_ctx` sized to fit, design §2.6);
  - `max_output_tokens` 4k, the lowest effort the model allows;
  - the feature defaults to a small model from the per-release data file (design §3.2);
  - "≈ $x" before the run (`estimate_generation`);
  - usually 1–2 runs per course per term.
- **Concurrency and cancel:** at most one calendar run per course (otherwise `busy`, design §3.7).
  `cancel_generation(id)` (design §3.3) is implemented from alpha.3, earlier than M3. Usage goes
  into `ai_usage`.

### 7.6 Assembly (`core::calendar::assemble`)

- Claims are sorted by date and grouped into segments: two `first_class` claims separated by a
  break of ≥ 3 weeks make the two segments of a Y course.
- The `weeks` table decides `first_week_number` (restart or continue in segment 2) and each break's
  `numbered` (a break that appears in the week table counts in the numbering).
- The result goes through V7 again.
- `resulting_week_today`, `resulting_phase` and the difference from the current accepted calendar
  are computed.

### 7.7 Injection defence (course text is untrusted data)

- **All of design §4.3 applies:** no tools (Codex tripwire, Claude `--tools ""`, empty HTTP
  `tools`), data tags, schema-constrained output, output never triggers an action.
- **New for this feature:**
  - **Verbatim quotes plus "the date must parse from the quote"** (V3, V4). An injected instruction
    can at most make the model quote a date that really is in the text; it can't make one up.
  - A false date written into the material ("Final exam: October 1") is the instructor's own text,
    trusted as much as the instructor. V7 (exam before last class → conflict), V8 (disagrees with
    materials) and the student's review catch it or flag it.
  - **A proposal has no action path.** `accept_calendar_proposal` writes only `course_calendars` and
    the mirrored `user_term_*`. `core::calendar` doesn't depend on any removal, hide or policy API;
    CAL-40 asserts that a run adds exactly one proposal row.
  - Labels and topics are plain text on every surface: no Markdown, no HTML, no links.
  - Titles in the structure block are instructor text too (the `timeline.rs` module docs) and sit
    inside the data tags.

### 7.8 Staleness (computed at read time, never stored)

- For each quote of the accepted calendar: its material's `content_hash` unchanged → OK; changed →
  look for the quote in the new chunks.
  - All found: still `Accepted`, with the evidence line "syllabus updated, dates unchanged".
  - Any missing: `AcceptedStale`, confidence Medium, "The syllabus changed on … — re-read?". The
    calendar **stays in force**: it is still the best evidence.
- New candidates only offer a run; they don't make the calendar stale.
- The scan proposes the difference after a sync; an AI re-read is the student's click.
- When a calendar changes the week numbering, saved explanations whose `generations.week_starts_on`
  no longer matches are marked `stale` (design §5.2).

## 8. Course lifecycle and removal

### 8.1 States and rules (`core::lifecycle`; computed at every read, never stored)

**Inputs:** the phase and `TermResolution` (segments, `exams_end`, outer frame); `lms_concluded`,
`lms_workflow_state`, `enrollment_active`; the session window; **last activity** = the latest of
material publish, announcement, event and module unlock dates on or before today; **next event**
= the earliest course event of any kind except `Other` from today to today + 30 days
(`EventKind`, `model.rs:403`); `keep_current_until`.

The first matching rule decides, with one exception: an Ended result of rules 2–3b becomes
Finishing when a next event exists (rule 4).

| Order | Rule | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `keep_current_until ≥ today` | Current, "you marked this course current until X" |
| 2 | any of: the calendar's `exams_end` + 7 < today (no exam date: last class + 28); `lms_concluded = true`; `lms_workflow_state = completed`; `enrollment_active = false` (after a complete course list); a plausible, unclipped LMS or folder end + 21 < today | **Ended · High** |
| 3 | only when the phase is Ended or Unknown (or comes from a Low anchor): end signal E1 (a non-student anchor gives phase Ended) or E2 (an implausible but past LMS `term_end`), **and** no activity in 21 days | **Ended · Medium** |
| 3b | the same phase condition: E3 (session window end + 21 < today) counts only together with E2 or "no activity in 60 days". The session hint alone never ends a course | Ended · Medium |
| 4 | the phase is ExamPeriod; or an end signal of rule 3/3b with activity in the last 21 days (e.g. grade announcements); or **any Ended result from rules 2–3b while a next event exists** (review finding 6) | Finishing; no removal suggestion |
| 5 | phase NotStarted, or the session window starts after today | Upcoming ("Starts Jan 11") |
| 6 | phase Teaching or Break | Current |
| 7 | no dates at all (no anchor, no window, no term) and no activity in 120 days (orientation, club and training sites on Quercus) | Inactive |
| 8 | otherwise | Unknown; with weak end evidence the detail page says "may have ended", but it doesn't go to Past |

- Rules 3 and 3b are skipped while the phase is Teaching or Break from an anchor of Medium or
  higher, so a Y course inside a January-ending Canvas term isn't ended by a quiet February.
- `suggest_removal` = Ended or Inactive, and `removal_snoozed_until` empty or past. Hidden courses
  are suggested too: hidden past courses are most of the backlog.
- **The owner's past courses:**
  - A Summer 2024 F course (window May–June 2024), a Winter 2025 S course (January–April 2025)
    and a Fall 2025 F course (September–December 2025) all meet E3.
  - Their Canvas term ends are probably past too (E2) **[unverified: the owner's data was not
    read]**, and they have been quiet for months.
  - So from alpha.1 they are Ended · Medium (High if Canvas says `concluded`) and go to Past.
- **The Fall 2026 courses:** Ended · High about 12-30 with an accepted calendar; with the A15
  calendar only, 21 quiet days after exams; with nothing, after 2027-02-01 (E2).
- **"Active"** (M3 uses it; `core::lifecycle::is_active`): Current, Finishing or Unknown, plus
  Upcoming courses that start within 14 days.

### 8.2 Timely without surprises

1. The lifecycle is recomputed at every read, so it changes the day a date passes, sync or not.
2. **Automatic, reversible, deletes nothing** (D43): Ended and Inactive go to the collapsed Past
   group; Ended, Inactive and Upcoming courses leave the week-based views: the digest's per-course
   week section, the default scope of study plans, the default week of explanations and the MCP
   weekly prompts. **Deadlines are not filtered by lifecycle** (review finding 6): `deadline_soon`,
   the digest's "deadlines in the next 7 days" and `list_deadlines` cover every visible, not
   removed course, so a real due date is never swallowed by a wrong "Ended".
3. **Banner:** when new courses become removal suggestions, the Courses page shows "3 courses look
   finished — Review". `lifecycle_summary()` computes it, so Tauri, Swift and the CLI agree
   (plan §1.3). "Not now" silences the same set for 14 days (`snooze_lifecycle_banner` sets
   `lifecycle_banner_snoozed_until`; `snooze_removal_suggestions` with `NotNow`); "Keep" stops
   suggesting a course for good. The facade computes every date.
4. **Removal needs the student's confirmation.** Stage 1 takes effect **at once**: the course
   leaves every list, the AI app and the digest, and sync never brings it back. Local data is
   purged only after 7 days, with one-click undo until then.
5. Files go to the system Trash at purge time (D44). The tombstone keeps a settings snapshot, and
   "Removed courses" always has a restore entry.

### 8.3 Removal in two stages

**Stage 1 `remove_courses`** (immediate; one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction)
1. Take `sync.lock`, or return `Busy`, as `remove_source` does (`lib.rs:550-567`). Otherwise a
   running sync could upsert the course again (`crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/sync.rs:862`) or
   recreate its folder.
2. Call `remember_course_names` (`lib.rs:557`); later alias refreshes skip tombstoned courses.
3. Write the tombstone: `state = pending`, `purge_after = now + 7 days`, the settings snapshot.
4. Set `courses.hidden = 1`, so v3 readers skip it too. New readers also exclude tombstoned
   courses, so it shows under "Removed courses", not under "Hidden".
5. With `purge_now`, stage 2 follows at once, and the completion message offers no undo: "Deleted.
   Restoring means syncing it again" (review finding P8).

**Stage 2 `purge_removed_courses`** (at the next sync start or app start after it is due, or on
"Delete now"; holds `sync.lock`; database first, files second)

| What | How |
|---|---|
| the course's Canvas assignment and planner events (ids `<source>/assignment/…`, `<source>/planner/…`, `map.rs:5-6, 260, 319`) | **explicit** `DELETE FROM events WHERE course_id = ? AND source_id = <its source>`. Otherwise `ON DELETE SET NULL` leaves them unlinked: assignment events then survive every later sync (`sync.rs:322-325`) and planner events survive until the next complete planner read (`sync.rs:314-320`; `map::planner_item` doesn't re-import them, `map.rs:310`), so they show up in the global deadline list and MCP `list_deadlines` (`views.rs:455-466`) |
| the `courses` row, with cascading modules, materials, chunks and `chunks_fts` | deleted (`store.rs:58-132`; the FTS triggers stay consistent) |
| `course_calendars`, v4 `generations` | cascade |
| calendar-feed events linked to the course | become unlinked; views hide events whose `course_hint` matches a tombstoned code |
| `study_plans` JSON | unchanged in the database; the app and MCP filter at read time |
| **FTS residue** (review findings 18, P5) | the delete trigger only adds FTS5 delete keys; the old terms stay in older segments until a merge [S4]. After the deletes: `INSERT INTO chunks_fts(chunks_fts) VALUES('optimize')`, then `PRAGMA secure_delete = ON` for this connection only and a best-effort `wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)`, so freed pages are zeroed |
| the pre-update backup `pagelamp.db.v<N>.bak` (design §5.4) | still holds the course's text. The removal dialog offers "Also delete the pre-update backup"; ticked by default when the backup is ≥ 14 days old, unticked (with the reason: it is the way back from a bad update) when newer. The facade computes both (`RemovalPreview.backup`). PRIVACY says so |
| files PageLamp downloaded from Canvas: the current `<CODE>-<id>`, older-code `…-<id>` folders (`course_dir_suffix`, `crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/lib.rs:216`), the parent folders of materials' `local_path`, `.part` leftovers | moved to the **system Trash** (D44). **The Trash** (review findings 19, P8)
- macOS uses the `trash` crate's `NsFileManager` method: the default Finder method "might ask the
  user to give additional permissions" and plays the Finder sound [S5]. The crate is MIT;
  `cargo deny` checks it.
- An **automatic** purge (sync start, app start) never deletes permanently. If the Trash fails, the
  files stay, `files_pending = 1`, and "Removed courses" plus `startup_tasks` say so.
- Permanent deletion happens only when the student clicks "Delete permanently" after a Trash
  failure (`permanent_if_no_trash = true`).

**What a later sync can't bring back** (listed in the dialog)
- announcements older than 120 days (`ANNOUNCEMENT_DAYS`, `crates/pagelamp-canvas/src/sync.rs:42`);
- files the course locks after it ends **[unverified]**;
- the whole course when Canvas lists it with `access_restricted_by_date` (`lms_access_restricted`,
  S2): such a course is never upserted again (`map.rs:54-56`), so its preview row says "can't be
  synced again" (review finding 15);
- a re-download counts as viewing in Canvas.

### 8.4 Tombstones and sync

- A tombstone matches `(source_id, external_id)`. Canvas: S6, S7, S8; folders and feeds: §5.
- Sync skips `pending` and `purged` tombstones and **does not** skip `restoring` ones (review
  finding 16).
- Removing a whole source deletes its tombstones by cascade; adding the source again brings every
  course back (documented).
- Known limit: renaming a folder changes the course id and gets around the tombstone. The UI says
  "PageLamp ignores <folder name>; your folder is not changed".

### 8.5 Restore, forget, removed list

| Tombstone state | `restore_course` |
|---|---|
| `pending` (within 7 days) | delete the tombstone, put back the previous `hidden`; immediate, nothing lost |
| `purged` | the tombstone becomes `restoring`, then the source is synced through the facade's internal sync entry that doesn't take `sync.lock` again (restore already holds it). When the course is upserted: `settings_json` is applied, the accepted calendar's dates become a calendar with origin `restored`, and the tombstone is deleted. Canvas files come back as `NotDownloaded`. When the sync **doesn't** upsert the course (not listed, access restricted, network failure), the tombstone goes back to `purged` and the result names the reason: "Canvas no longer lists this course", "Canvas restricts access to this course", "offline" |
| folder source | re-indexed from the student's own files |

- **Forget** is allowed only for `purged` tombstones: it deletes the tombstone, and the confirmation
  says "the next sync brings it back". A `pending` removal offers Undo instead, which restores the
  previous `hidden` (review finding 16).
- Entry points: Settings → Data → "Removed courses"; the data summary (`DataSection.tsx`) gains a
  removed count; CLI `course removed`.

### 8.6 Concurrency and privacy

- **Lock.** `remove_courses`, `purge_removed_courses` and `restore_course` hold `sync.lock`; during
  a sync they return `Busy` and the UI says "Sync in progress".
- **Write order.** Database changes in one short `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction; at purge the
  database commits first, then files are moved, and S8 sweeps what a failure leaves. The reverse
  order could leave "files gone, course still there".
- **MCP readers** open a read-only connection per request (`crates/pagelamp-mcp/src/lib.rs:924`).
  During a removal a request gets at worst an empty result or NotFound, never corrupt data.
- **Privacy.**
  - The tombstone keeps the course code and name until "Forget";
  - `course-aliases.json` entries expire after 30 days as today (PRIVACY);
  - the settings snapshot holds no quotes.

### 9.3 How the course lane works (D41)

The course-calendar work lands on its own branch, so its unfinished work never mixes with the
rest of `main`. So:

- **Own worktree and branch.** backend and frontend work in a second worktree on branch
  `feat/course-lane`, with the same pathspec rules between them as on `main` (backend `crates/
  apps/pagelamp-cli/`, frontend `apps/desktop/`). backend and frontend stay on `main`.
- **New code in new files.** `pagelamp-core/src/{term,lifecycle,dates}.rs`,
  `pagelamp-core/src/calendar/`, `pagelamp-app/src/course/`, the CLI `course` subcommand module,
  `apps/desktop/src/features/course/{timeline,removal}/`. Shared files (`store.rs`, `model.rs`,
  `views.rs`, `pagelamp-mcp/src/lib.rs`, `pagelamp-app/src/lib.rs` re-exports, CLI `main.rs`) get
  small, separate commits.
- **One migration author.** backend writes the v3 and v4 migrations (M0.8, M1) from the lane's
  DDL spec (§3), so schema versions never fork.
- **Merging.** The maintainer merges `feat/course-lane` into `main` at least weekly and before each
  alpha tag, resolves conflicts with both backend owners, and runs the full DoD.
- **Disk.** The second worktree has its own build output. Disk space is not a constraint (286 GB
  free on 2026-09-28), but the standing rule applies: delete `target/` after each build's checks.
- The lane works on its own branch (D41, decided yes).
- **If D41 is declined,** the lane stays in the shared tree: new code only in new files, and every
  edit to a shared file goes through backend (§9.5).

Two candidate designs were compared: "evidence first" (E) and "calendar first" (C). E is the base;
four things came from C (the `CourseCalendar` structure with Y segments and numbered breaks;
two-stage removal; AI reading in alpha.3; the MCP proposal path). Rows changed by the review are
marked.

| # | Question | E | C | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | which schema version gets the new tables | all in v3 | tombstones and calendars in v4 | v3 only adds raw Canvas columns and student columns; new tables in v4 (compatibility step, `generations` FK) |
| 2 | meaning of `courses.term_start` | term only | unchanged | unchanged, plus raw `lms_*` columns |
| 3 | plausibility | reject > 20 weeks | > 26 weeks, or start > 28 days before activity | 26 weeks (36 for Y) + activity + window clauses + 4-week minimum; **plus the end clause (review)** |
| 4 | timetable first class | priority 2 | priority 6 | **cross-check only in v0.3.0 (review)** |
| 5 | institution hint | month windows as bounds + exact A15 calendar | month windows giving a Low week | E; the hint alone never ends a course (from C) |
| 6 | materials fit | latest 3 weeks, median, deviation | median of all | E, **with per-day max, earliest per week, weekend shift (review)** |
| 7 | `current_week` in a break | last teaching week | None | None for unnumbered breaks, plus `break_after_week` and `default_week` |
| 8 | model output | flat claims | structured segments | flat claims + C's `not_found`, assembled by core; **plus `header_quote` (review)** |
